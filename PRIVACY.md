# PortOS for iOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

PortOS for iOS (technical project name: PortDeck) is a companion for PortOS instances that you own and operate. ShadowPuppet, LLC does not operate a PortOS cloud service for this app and does not receive your app data.

## Data handling

- PortOS instance addresses, labels, and connection state are stored on your device.
- PortOS passwords are stored in the Apple Keychain. If you explicitly enable iCloud sync, fleet profiles and passwords are stored as synchronizable Keychain items in your iCloud account.
- Brain entries, Daily Log entries, action parameters, and other content are sent only to the PortOS instance you select.
- Dictation currently uses Apple's on-device speech recognition and sends the resulting text to the selected PortOS instance. PortOS for iOS does not retain a recording archive.
- The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, or a developer-operated account system.

The app includes an offline demo containing fictional data. Demo mode does not contact the network, Keychain, or iCloud.

## Your choices

You can remove a saved PortOS instance from the Fleet screen. You can disable iCloud synchronization from Settings at any time. Content stored by a PortOS instance is managed and deleted on that user-owned instance according to its configuration.

## Future audio support

A future opt-in feature may upload audio directly to a PortOS instance selected and operated by the user. It will not upload audio to a ShadowPuppet-operated service. This policy will be updated before that feature is released.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, open an issue at <https://github.com/atomantic/PortOS/issues>.
